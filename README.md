# Bird Flight Directions

A field page for counting birds in flight from one vantage point: tap or swipe on a compass to record which side
each bird or flock came from and which way it went, then see the flight paths on a map.

**Open it:** https://songpon123.github.io/bird-flight-directions/

- Works in any phone or computer browser, no account needed. Add it to your home screen to use it like an app.
- GPS for your position, phone compass, and satellite, street or terrain map backgrounds.
- Vantage point survey fields: Act/Opp record type, bearing (degree) and distance to the bird, and flight height
  every 15 seconds up to 180 s with a beeping timer.
- Survey tab for watch periods and hourly weather (cloud, wind, precipitation, visibility, temperature), with CSV
  exports laid out like the paper VP bird and weather record sheets.
- "ID by shape" helper: pick a flight-silhouette group (raptors, geese, cranes, storks, cormorants and more) to see
  field marks and the species recorded in China or Thailand.
- Reads and writes eBird species-comment text such as `1342 12 E to W；1413 5 E to NW 150m`.
- Data stays in your own browser. Back up to JSON and share it (Google Drive, LINE, email) from the Data tab;
  importing a backup merges records without duplicates.

Species list: eBird/Clements 2025 taxonomy for China, Hong Kong, Taiwan and Thailand, derived from
[Scythebill](https://bitbucket.org/scythebill/scythebill-birdlist) (Apache 2.0, see `species-NOTICE.txt`).
